Paolo Micera

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore in Milan, working under the supervision of Claudio Lucifora and Michele Belloni.

Currently, I am a visiting researcher at the University of Groningen, under the supervision of Viola Angelini and Max Groneck.

My research is in labor, health and household economics. I study how health capital is formed over the life cycle and how health shocks shape individual and family decisions, using administrative and survey microdata.
